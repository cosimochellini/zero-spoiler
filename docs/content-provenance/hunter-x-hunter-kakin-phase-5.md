# Kakin phase 5 inventory

Window: chapters 401–420, the last published chapter (#1138). All records and
dated additions are manga-only. Episode 148 orders them and never reveals them
to anime readers. Chapter synopses were read through the lawful English Hunter x
Hunter Wiki MediaWiki API (`action=query&prop=revisions`), at the revisions
listed below. Cast lists and first-appearance fields identify candidates only.

Manga pages, exact page numbers and licensed Italian manga spellings were not
viewed. Italian names come from Italian Wikipedia, read through its API on
2026-11-06. Original neutral placeholders reuse phase 4's person drawing;
finished drawings remain #917 and chronicles #918.

## How a gate was read

A gate is the first chapter in the window whose synopsis puts the name in
someone's quoted or reported speech, a broadcast, a letter or a
self-introduction. Narration, a cast list and a name used only in narrated
thought are not gates. A name the synopsis writes for the person being spoken to
("Belerainte says that he did not expect Naipei to be targeted", said to Naipei)
stands for "you" and is not a gate either. Earlier chapters that later wiki
infoboxes give as a name chapter (Rihan 374, Musse 366, Shikaku 376, Sumidori
386, Balsamilco 363) were audited by phases 2–4 without an explicit signal; the
records open at the later, verified scene. Late is safe.

A record whose surname is not said in the window is filed under the name that
is: Balsamilco, Hinrigh, Morena and Onior. `TITLES` in
`scripts/verify-chapters.tables.mjs` points each at its wiki page. Oito is filed
as Oito Hui Guo Rou like the other queens: Hui Guo Rou is the royal family's
name, said with the roster at 349.

Musse, Shikaku and Risnorth are already dead at their opening chapter, and
Kanjidol is tied up at his (415). No earlier live stage is invented. Every fact
carries `source: 'chapter'`, episode 148 and an explicit chapter.

Changes not pinned: Tserriednich's death at 417 is staged, and the reader is
told the plan at 416, so his status stays alive. Camilla, Tubeppa and Tyson are
infected with TSK-17 but alive at 420. Zhang Lei and Luzurus flee before martial
law, and the soldiers report how; they are not lost to the story. The deaths of
Luini (405) and Moswana (416) are not filed because neither has a record.
Sumidori's infobox lists him as deceased, but no death is told in the window, so
he stays alive.

## Relationships

Each new character's selections name only characters open at that chapter.
Hinrigh's exchange is with Zakuro at 406 and Morena's with Borksen at 407, after
their gates. Risnorth has no exchange with a filed character (he is dead and
shown only in a photograph) and is listed under `omitted` in
`hunter-x-hunter-relationships.json`. Maizan stays omitted: his 393 exchange
with Hinrigh is before Hinrigh's gate.

## Places

No place is filed. The window stays on the Black Whale, already filed: its
tiers, the numbered rooms, the VIP casino and movie theater, the Justice
Bureau's prosecution and courthouse area, the Tier 3 hospital, Nasubi's quarters
and burial chamber, the Heil-Ly hideout and Room 302 are descriptive parts of
the ship and are not filed, as the owner ruled. The Royal Military Academy
(401), the Kakin Empire (408, 417), the New Continent and the Heavens Arena
(406, 408) are only mentioned. Makaha's and Bonolenov's flashbacks and Morena's
account of the carnival orphans' institution name no place.

## Italian names

Read on 2026-11-06 through the Italian Wikipedia API:
`Personaggi di Hunter × Hunter` (revision 153230591), `Associazione Hunter`
(153173616), `Famiglia Zoldick` (153144759) and `Brigata Fantasma` (153145307).
The character list gives Oito Hoicoro, Morena Prudo and Onior Longbao; Oito
takes the Italian form, Morena and Onior keep the name said in the window, which
is part of the listed one. The other 19 names are absent from all four pages and
are kept in English in `scripts/verify-names.kakin.mjs`.
