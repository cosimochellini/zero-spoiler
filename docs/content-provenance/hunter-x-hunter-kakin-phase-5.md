# Kakin phase 5 inventory

Window: chapters 401–420, the last published chapter (#1138). All records and
dated additions are manga-only. Episode 148 orders them and never reveals them
to anime readers. Chapter synopses were read through the lawful English Hunter x
Hunter Wiki MediaWiki API (`action=query&prop=revisions`), at the revisions
listed below. Cast lists and first-appearance fields identify candidates only.

Manga pages, exact page numbers and licensed Italian manga spellings were not
viewed. Italian names come from Italian Wikipedia, read through its API on
2026-11-06. The records are drawn in #1158 (part of #917). Chronicles remain
#918.

## Naming checks (#1145)

[#1145](https://github.com/cosimochellini/zero-spoiler/issues/1145) gives every
candidate the phase deferred a disposition. None is filed, because the owner
found no naming balloon or caption in any of the scenes asked about below.

**The owner's naming rule (2026-11-26).** A character is named only when the
name is said in a speech balloon or printed beside the character (a caption or
an introduction box, as in #833). A name said to the listener counts ("Naipei,
come here!"). These do not count: a thought balloon, a nickname that differs
from the record's name ("Dog" is not "Dogman"), the wiki's narration or
synopsis, cast lists and images alone. This replaces "reported speech or
thought" in the issue body. A candidate with no proof stays deferred or is
omitted, with its reason, and the issue still closes. Kakin is manga-only, so
any later record is `source: 'chapter'`.

**How it was checked.** The pinned chapter synopses and the candidates' wiki
pages pointed to the window's scenes where a name could be said. The owner
checked those scenes and answered each one "no" (owner-accepted, VIZ page not
viewed). Scenes the synopses do not point to were not asked about. No manga
mirror, scan or fan transcript was used (#725).
