# Chimera Ant phase 2 inventory

**Scope**

- Chapters 199–222 and episodes 85–95. The phase also files phase 1's leftovers,
  names that are said in chapters 186–198 / episodes 76–84 but had not been
  checked (#1020).
- Sources: the English Hunter x Hunter Wiki chapter, episode and character
  pages, read through the MediaWiki API at the revisions pinned in
  `sources.json`, and Italian Wikipedia for the Italian names. Manga panels,
  licensed episode frames and timestamps, and the Italian dub audio were not
  viewed.

**Gate rule (owner ruling, 2026-11-03)**

- A medium's gate is the first chapter or episode whose synopsis gives an
  explicit naming signal: an introduction, "named", the name in someone's quoted
  or reported speech or printed thought, or a caption.
- Narration alone does not count, so that medium waits for an explicit signal.
- A name that only one medium gives makes a source-only record, and its other
  number only orders it.
- The lower points that rest on narration alone are listed under "Owner checks",
  so a later pull request can lower a gate once the page or episode is checked.

**Names in the synopses but not filed**

- Five Squadron Leaders are only ever mentioned, never seen: Reikei, Goran, Zem,
  Pokoro and Gaftz.
- Chiona is seen unnamed and is otherwise only mentioned.
- Owner ruling: none of them is filed.
