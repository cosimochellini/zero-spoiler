## Evidence and corrections

Each entry’s independent episode/chapter evidence and final corrections are
recorded in [the chronicle verification log](../chronicle-verification.md) and
the HxH source map. Consulted MediaWiki revisions are pinned in `sources.json`;
no scans, frames, dialogue or subtitles are committed. Text is independently
authored in English and Italian. Direct licensed pages, video and dub audio were
not viewed.

Corrections include premature names and markers, anime/manga-specific
descriptions, Italian dub spellings, Colt/Hagya source ordering, Komugi’s
inactive coordinate, Knuckle’s chapter-276 pursuit, the chapter-290 extraction,
and the timing of Pouf’s core and Youpi’s poison explanation. No later Komugi
death confirmation or Zodiac identity is inferred at the arc endpoint.
