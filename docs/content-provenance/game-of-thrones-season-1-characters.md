# Game of Thrones, season 1 characters: research notes

Research for #852: the main cast of season 1 and _A Game of Thrones_, 63
characters. Read through the MediaWiki APIs of the lawful sources only (#725): A
Wiki of Ice and Fire for the books, the Game of Thrones Wiki on Fandom for the
show, and Italian Wikipedia for the Italian dub's names, all CC BY-SA. No text
is copied from them.

- **Gates.** A record opens where the character appears and is named, in each
  medium. The site's chapter is absolute: the book's prologue is chapter 1, so
  the wiki's "Chapter N" of _A Game of Thrones_ is site chapter N + 1.
- **Dated steps** stop at the end of stage 1 (episode 10, chapter 73) and pin
  both numbers. A step one medium alone tells carries `source`.
- **Not viewed.** Neither the episodes nor the books were viewed for this
  research. Every gate or step the wikis leave open is listed under `unverified`
  in `game-of-thrones.json`, for the owner's hand check.

## The Starks and their direwolves

Site chapter = AGOT chapter + 1 (Prologue = 1). Chapter appearances come from
each AWOIAF chapter page's "Character list" (Appearing / Mentioned) and
synopsis; episode facts from the Fandom episode synopses and character pages.

All six direwolves appear as unnamed pups in Bran I (site 2) and in "Winter Is
Coming". In each medium, a wolf's gate is the first chapter or episode where it
appears **and** is named. Fandom gives episode 1 as every wolf's first
appearance, but no transcript confirms the names are said there, so each show
gate is the first episode whose synopsis calls the wolf by name: Lady 1 (she
dies in episode 2, so her gate cannot move there), Nymeria 2, Summer 4, Ghost 4,
Grey Wind 8, Shaggydog 10. That errs towards fog. The owner's hand check may
lower any of them.

### Eddard Stark

- Episode 1, "Winter Is Coming": he beheads the deserter and is addressed as
  Lord Stark and Ned.
- Chapter 2, Bran I (POV Bran): the same execution, then the pups.
- Italian: Eddard Stark (it.wikipedia `Eddard_Stark`, TV section).
- Role: Lord of Winterfell (1, 2), then Hand of the King (2, 7). Book: Catelyn
  II (site 7), he decides to accept after Lysa's letter. Show: he accepts at the
  end of episode 1, which is the gate, so the step goes to episode 2, where the
  synopsis calls him the new Hand on the Kingsroad. Unverified: owner to
  confirm.
- Affiliation: House Stark (1, 2); House Stark, small council (3, 21). Show
  "Lord Snow": he is called to a small council meeting as soon as he reaches
  King's Landing. Book: Eddard IV (site 21), the same.
- Status: captured (7, 50). "You Win or You Die": the City Watch turns on him in
  the throne room and Littlefinger holds a knife to his throat. Book: Eddard XIV
  (site 50), the same. Deceased (9, 66). "Baelor": beheaded after his
  confession. Book: Arya V (site 66).
- Not filed: his resignation as Hand and reinstatement (one episode in the show,
  episode 5; Eddard VIII to Eddard X in the book), as the role is the same
  before and after.

- No dated steps. The capture of Tyrion (ep 4 / Catelyn V) changes nothing on

### Robb Stark

- Role: Heir to Winterfell (1, 2); King in the North (10, 72). "Fire and Blood":
  his bannermen proclaim him. Book: Catelyn XI (site 72), Greatjon Umber's

### Sansa Stark

- Chapter 5, Eddard I (POV Ned): appears in the welcome in the yard. The chapter

## Winterfell’s household and the Wall

Method. Book gates come from the "Appearing:" list on each AWOIAF chapter page
(prologue + chapters 1–72, site chapter = N + 1), checked against the chapter
synopsis. Show gates come from each Fandom page's `First` field and Season 1
section, plus the season-1 episode synopses. Fandom has no transcripts, so for
most show gates **who says the name aloud is not settled**; each such gate is in
`unverified`. Italian names: Italian Wikipedia gives the same form for book and
dub here (the sections note the dub actors, no variant names). `name.it` keeps
the bare form when Fandom's title is bare (Luwin, Mordane, Aemon), as the
brief's examples do. No record needs `commonWord`.

## The royal family, the Lannisters, the Vale and the Riverlands

Site chapter = AGOT chapter + 1 (Prologue = 1). Episode gates start from
Fandom's `First`; chapter gates come from the AWOIAF chapter pages' "Appearing"
lists and synopses, checked against `{{Ref|AGOT|N}}` on the character pages.

### Cersei Lannister

- Episode 1, "Winter Is Coming": arrives at Winterfell with the king. Fandom
  First = Winter Is Coming. The exact line that names her was not checked
  (unverified).

  wheelhouse. She is only mentioned in chapters 3–4.

### Tyrion Lannister
