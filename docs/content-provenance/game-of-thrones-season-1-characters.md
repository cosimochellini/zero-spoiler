# Game of Thrones, season 1 characters: research notes

Research for #852: the main cast of season 1 and _A Game of Thrones_, 63
characters. Read through the MediaWiki APIs of the lawful sources only (#725): A
Wiki of Ice and Fire for the books, the Game of Thrones Wiki on Fandom for the
show, and Italian Wikipedia for the Italian dub's names, all CC BY-SA. No text
is copied from them.

- **Gates.** A record opens where the character appears and is named, in each
  medium. The site's chapter is absolute: the book's prologue is chapter 1, so
  the wiki's "Chapter N" of _A Game of Thrones_ is site chapter N + 1.
- **Dated steps** stop at the end of stage 1 (episode 10, chapter 73) and pin
  both numbers. A step one medium alone tells carries `source`.
- **Not viewed.** Neither the episodes nor the books were viewed for this
  research. Every gate or step the wikis leave open is listed under `unverified`
  in `game-of-thrones.json`, for the owner's hand check.

## The Starks and their direwolves

Site chapter = AGOT chapter + 1 (Prologue = 1). Chapter appearances come from
each AWOIAF chapter page's "Character list" (Appearing / Mentioned) and
synopsis; episode facts from the Fandom episode synopses and character pages.

All six direwolves appear as unnamed pups in Bran I (site 2) and in "Winter Is
Coming". In each medium, a wolf's gate is the first chapter or episode where it
appears **and** is named. Fandom gives episode 1 as every wolf's first
appearance, but no transcript confirms the names are said there, so each show
gate is the first episode whose synopsis calls the wolf by name: Lady 1 (she
dies in episode 2, so her gate cannot move there), Nymeria 2, Summer 4, Ghost 4,
Grey Wind 8, Shaggydog 10. That errs towards fog. The owner's hand check may
lower any of them.

### Eddard Stark

- Episode 1, "Winter Is Coming": he beheads the deserter and is addressed as
  Lord Stark and Ned.
- Chapter 2, Bran I (POV Bran): the same execution, then the pups.
- Italian: Eddard Stark (it.wikipedia `Eddard_Stark`, TV section).
- Role: Lord of Winterfell (1, 2), then Hand of the King (2, 7). Book: Catelyn
  II (site 7), he decides to accept after Lysa's letter. Show: he accepts at the
  end of episode 1, which is the gate, so the step goes to episode 2, where the
  synopsis calls him the new Hand on the Kingsroad. Unverified: owner to
  confirm.
- Affiliation: House Stark (1, 2); House Stark, small council (3, 21). Show
  "Lord Snow": he is called to a small council meeting as soon as he reaches
  King's Landing. Book: Eddard IV (site 21), the same.
- Status: captured (7, 50). "You Win or You Die": the City Watch turns on him in
  the throne room and Littlefinger holds a knife to his throat. Book: Eddard XIV
  (site 50), the same. Deceased (9, 66). "Baelor": beheaded after his
  confession. Book: Arya V (site 66).
- Not filed: his resignation as Hand and reinstatement (one episode in the show,
  episode 5; Eddard VIII to Eddard X in the book), as the role is the same
  before and after.

- Episode 1: she brings her husband the news of Jon Arryn's death and the king's

- Chapter 3, Catelyn I (POV Catelyn): the same scene. Mentioned only in Bran I.
- Italian: Catelyn Stark (it.wikipedia `Catelyn_Stark`, which heads "Catelyn
  Stark, nata Tully"). The dub form is assumed.
- No dated steps. The capture of Tyrion (ep 4 / Catelyn V) changes nothing on
  her own record.

### Robb Stark

- Chapter 2, Bran I: rides to the execution and holds the first pup.
- Italian: Robb Stark (it.wikipedia `Casa_Stark#Robb_Stark`).
- Role: Heir to Winterfell (1, 2); King in the North (10, 72). "Fire and Blood":
  his bannermen proclaim him. Book: Catelyn XI (site 72), Greatjon Umber's
  speech and the proclamation.

### Sansa Stark

- Episode 1: at her needlework and in the yard for the king.
- Chapter 5, Eddard I (POV Ned): appears in the welcome in the yard. The chapter

## Winterfell’s household and the Wall

Method. Book gates come from the "Appearing:" list on each AWOIAF chapter page
(prologue + chapters 1–72, site chapter = N + 1), checked against the chapter
synopsis. Show gates come from each Fandom page's `First` field and Season 1
section, plus the season-1 episode synopses. Fandom has no transcripts, so for
most show gates **who says the name aloud is not settled**; each such gate is in
`unverified`. Italian names: Italian Wikipedia gives the same form for book and
dub here (the sections note the dub actors, no variant names). `name.it` keeps
the bare form when Fandom's title is bare (Luwin, Mordane, Aemon), as the
brief's examples do. No record needs `commonWord`.

## The royal family, the Lannisters, the Vale and the Riverlands

Site chapter = AGOT chapter + 1 (Prologue = 1). Episode gates start from
Fandom's `First`; chapter gates come from the AWOIAF chapter pages' "Appearing"
lists and synopses, checked against `{{Ref|AGOT|N}}` on the character pages.

### Cersei Lannister

- Episode 1, "Winter Is Coming": arrives at Winterfell with the king. Fandom
  First = Winter Is Coming. The exact line that names her was not checked
  (unverified).
- Chapter 5, Eddard I (POV Ned): Ned bows as Queen Cersei steps down from the
  wheelhouse. She is only mentioned in chapters 3–4.
- Italian: Cersei Lannister (it.wikipedia `Cersei_Lannister`).
- Role step: (7, 50) queen → mother of the new king. Ep 7 has Robert's death and

### Tyrion Lannister

- Episode 1: "You're Tyrion Lannister? The Queen's brother?" (Fandom quote).

## King’s Landing

Site chapter = AGOT chapter + 1 (Prologue = 1). Book first appearances come from
each AWOIAF chapter page's "Appearing" list, then the synopsis for the naming
line. Show first appearances come from the Fandom infobox `First` and the
season-1 biography `{{Ref|GOT1xx}}` citations. Italian names: every character
keeps the same name in Italian (it.wikipedia, pages listed below).

### Ilyn Payne

- Episode 2, "The Kingsroad". Fandom `First`. At the Inn at the Crossroads the
  Hound introduces Sansa to Ser Ilyn Payne, the king's headsman whose tongue the
  Mad King had torn out. Unverified: that the name is spoken aloud.
- Chapter 16, Sansa I (AGOT 15), Sansa POV. Ilyn rides in with Renly and
  Barristan, and Joffrey names him to Sansa as the King's Justice. Chapter 2
  (Bran I) only mentions him.
- Italian: "Ilyn Payne", it.wikipedia
  `Personaggi delle Cronache del ghiaccio e del fuoco#Ilyn Payne`.
- No dated steps. His beheading of Eddard in episode 9 and Eddard's in the books
  does not change his role.

### Barristan Selmy

- Episode 3, "Lord Snow". Fandom `First`. He trades war stories with Robert and
  Jaime in the Red Keep. Unverified: that he is named and called Lord Commander
  aloud.
- Chapter 16, Sansa I (AGOT 15). He introduces himself to Sansa as Lord
  Commander of the Kingsguard. Chapter 9 (Bran II) only mentions him.
- Italian: "Barristan Selmy",
  `Personaggi delle Cronache del ghiaccio e del fuoco#Barristan Selmy`.
- Step at (8, 58): Joffrey dismisses him from the Kingsguard. Show: "The Pointy
  End" (GOT108). Books: Sansa V (AGOT 57), Joffrey's first court. Role becomes
  "Knight dismissed from the Kingsguard"; affiliation becomes "Formerly of the
  Kingsguard".

### Syrio Forel

- Episode 3, "Lord Snow". Fandom `First`. Eddard hires him to teach Arya, and
  his lessons pass as dancing lessons.
- Chapter 23, Arya II (AGOT 22), Arya POV. Three days after the Needle talk,
  Arya meets him in the Small Hall: a former First Sword of Braavos and her new
  "dancing master".
- Italian: "Syrio Forel",
  `Personaggi delle Cronache del ghiaccio e del fuoco#Syrio Forel`.
- No death filed. In episode 8 and in Arya IV (site 51) he holds off the
  Lannister guards and Meryn Trant while Arya runs, and neither medium shows how
  it ends. Status stays alive from the gate. Unverified: that the books, like
  the show, call the lessons "dancing lessons" to the household.

### Petyr Baelish

- Episode 3, "Lord Snow". Fandom `First`. He greets Eddard, sits at the first
  council and receives Catelyn at a brothel.
- Chapter 19, Catelyn IV (AGOT 18), Catelyn POV. Catelyn is taken to him in
  secret, and he tells her the dagger was his, lost to Tyrion in a wager.
  Chapter 9 (Bran II) only mentions him.
- Italian: "Petyr Baelish" (nickname "Ditocorto"), it.wikipedia `Petyr Baelish`.
- No dated steps within stage 1. Unverified: that "Littlefinger" is said aloud
  in episode 3, and that the show says there he has known Catelyn since youth.

### Varys

- Episode 3, "Lord Snow". He greets Eddard at the council, then joins the
  meeting with Catelyn.
- Chapter 19, Catelyn IV (AGOT 18). He joins Petyr and Catelyn and asks to see
  the dagger. Chapter 13 (Eddard II) only mentions him.
- Italian: "Varys", it.wikipedia `Varys`. The title "Maestro dei sussurri" is
  it.wikipedia's form.
- No dated steps. I left "eunuch" out of the shared text: the books say it by
  chapter 19, but I could not confirm that episode 3 says it (Pycelle says it in
  episode 4).

### Pycelle

  (Catelyn I) only mentions him.
- Italian: "Pycelle",
  `Personaggi delle Cronache del ghiaccio e del fuoco#Pycelle`.

### Sandor Clegane

- Episode 4, "Cripples, Bastards, and Broken Things". Littlefinger tells Sansa
  about the brothers and names Sandor. **Unverified, owner check**: he is on
  screen from episode 1 (Fandom `First`: "Winter Is Coming"), and in episode 2
  he brings Sansa to Ilyn and brings back Mycah's body, but I could not confirm
  that "Sandor" or "the Hound" is said aloud before episode 4. If it is, lower
  the gate to that episode. The gate text holds at 1 too.
- Chapter 5, Eddard I (AGOT 4), Eddard POV. Eddard recognizes Sandor Clegane
  among the king's party at Winterfell. Unverified: that this chapter already
  marks him as Joffrey's guard. The text says he guards the prince, which the
  books make plain in Eddard III (site 17).
- Italian: "Sandor Clegane" (nickname "il Mastino"), it.wikipedia
  `Sandor Clegane`.
- Step at (8, 58): he is named to the Kingsguard in Barristan's place. Show:
  "The Pointy End" (GOT108). Books: Sansa V (AGOT 57). Role becomes "Member of
  the Kingsguard" (not "knight": he refuses knighthood); affiliation goes from
  House Lannister to Kingsguard.

### Janos Slynt

### Gendry

- Episode 4, "Cripples, Bastards, and Broken Things". Tobho Mott shows Eddard
  his apprentice and the helm.
- Chapter 28, Eddard VI (AGOT 27). The same forge visit. Mott names the boy
  Gendry.
- Italian: "Gendry", it.wikipedia `Casa Baratheon#Gendry`.
- Fandom's page title is "Gendry Baratheon". I filed `gendry` / "Gendry" because
  the surname is a later reveal (it.wikipedia and AWOIAF title him Gendry).
  **Owner decision.**
- Step at (10, 66), `source: 'episode'`: in "Fire and Blood" (GOT110) he is
  among Yoren's Night's Watch recruits with Arya. The books first put him among
  the recruits in ACOK Arya I (site 75), after stage 1. Chapter 66 (Arya V,

  step.
- The texts avoid naming Tobho Mott and Jon Arryn ("an armorer", "the previous
  Hand").

### Gregor Clegane

- Episode 4, "Cripples, Bastards, and Broken Things". At the Hand's tourney he
  kills Ser Hugh in a joust, and Littlefinger names him to Sansa as the Mountain
  That Rides and tells her how he burned his brother's face.
- Chapter 30, Sansa II (AGOT 29), Sansa POV. He thunders past at the tourney,
  named in her account, and kills Ser Hugh. That night the Hound himself tells
  her about the burning. Gregor is mentioned before this (Bran I and others),
  never present.
- How the two media name him differs: the show uses Littlefinger's story, the
  books use Sansa's narration and the Hound's own story. The shared text keeps
  only the joust, "the Mountain" and the burning over a toy.
- Italian: "Gregor Clegane" ("la Montagna che cavalca"),
  `Personaggi delle Cronache del ghiaccio e del fuoco#Gregor Clegane`.
- Step at (6, 44): Eddard strips him of lands and titles and sends Beric to
  bring him to justice. Show: "A Golden Crown" (GOT106, per Fandom's Gregor
  page; the episode page was not read). Books: Eddard XI (AGOT 43, site 44).
- The Loras joust (episode 5; books Eddard VII, site 31) comes after the chapter
  gate, so the gate text leaves it out.

### Loras Tyrell

- Episode 5, "The Wolf and the Lion". He rides against Gregor and gives Sansa a

  episode 3 does not open him.
- Chapter 30, Sansa II (AGOT 29). He jousts at the Hand's tourney and gives
  Sansa a red rose.
- Italian: "Loras Tyrell" ("Cavaliere dei Fiori"), it.wikipedia
  `Casa Tyrell#Loras Tyrell`.
- No dated steps. Leaving the city with Renly (episode 7 / Eddard XIII) was not
  filed: it changes neither role nor affiliation.
