# Kakin phase 3 inventory

Window: chapters 371–383. All thirteen chapter synopses were read through the
lawful English Wiki MediaWiki API. Manga panels, page numbers and Italian manga
forms were not viewed. Every new record is manga-only at episode 148. Finished
art belongs to #917 and chronicles to #918.

## Places

The window visits numbered quarters, the banquet hall, the lifeboat area, the
ship’s courts and police station, the central dining hall and mafia offices.
These are descriptive subdivisions of the ship without independently verified
proper place names in the read synopses. Destinations, and the previously
visited Meteor City mentioned in dialogue, do not become newly visited places
here. No flashback in this window establishes a newly visited named place.

## Returning facts for original dossiers

- Momoze’s assassination at chapter 368 precedes this window; her body is placed
  in a capsule at chapter 371.
- Musse dies at chapter 373 when Camilla’s ability resurrects her.
- Tuffdy’s death is confirmed by Kurapika at chapter 374, after the
  confrontation at chapter 372.
- Myuhan’s death is revealed at chapter 376.
- Illumi introduces himself as the Troupe’s newest member at chapter 377.
- Salé-salé’s assassination succeeds at chapter 382.
- Kacho and Keeney die at chapter 383. The figure accompanying Fugetsu
  afterwards is Kacho’s Nen beast, not evidence that Kacho survives.
- Neon’s death is not filed from the inference that Chrollo’s stolen ability
  disappeared.
