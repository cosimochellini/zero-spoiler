Chrollo first uses Fun Fun Cloth at episode 52 / chapter 99 and Lovely
Ghostwriter at episode 54 / chapter 104. His user links still wait for his
character gate.

Chapter 54 shows Hisoka’s cards and the apparent repair of his arm. Chapter 55
explains the two techniques, so the opening records depict only the visible
effects. In chapter 97, the window opens before the fish vanish and the book
closes. The book’s own closing rule is explained in chapter 99.

## Other candidates

- True Tiger Bite Fist is prepared in episode 31 / chapter 53. The coordinated
  strike is interrupted in episode 32 / chapter 54. The solo Tiger Bite Fist is
  filed.
- The chapter 74 vault flashback shows the result of Owl's work. Episode 45 /
  chapter 77 shows the cloth shrinking its target.
- Kastro's Double, Sadaso's Aura Arm, Pakunoda's Psychometry, Melody's
  Enchanting Music, Squala's dog control, Silva's orbs, Illumi's disguise and
  corpse control, and Chrollo's Teleportation are wiki descriptions.
- Song of Defense and Thunder Snake are uses of Riehlvelt's whips. A Field in
  Spring is a musical piece. Gyo and In are shared Nen techniques.
- Steal Chain, Stealth Dolphin, Needle People, Dragon Dive, Pain Packer, Rising
  Sun, Ripper Cyclotron and Battle Cantabile first demonstrate their named
  functions in later arcs.
