# Kakin phase 4 inventory

Window: chapters 384–400. All records and dated additions are manga-only.
Episode 148 orders them and never reveals them to anime readers. Chapter
summaries were read through the lawful English Hunter x Hunter Wiki MediaWiki
API. Cast lists and first-appearance fields identify candidates only.

Manga pages, exact page numbers and licensed Italian manga spellings were not
viewed. English character names are retained in Italian pending verified forms.
Original neutral placeholders follow Election; finished drawings and redrawings
remain #917. Chronicles remain #918.

## Dated changes within the phase

- Sarasa is alive at her introduction in the childhood flashback (395); her body
  is found in 397. The death is pinned to 397.
- Sheila participates in the children’s group in 395 and leaves it in 397. Her
  origin is Meteor City, learned in the same flashback.
- Padaille (391), Misha Hao (392) and Tassi (394) are already known to be
  deceased by their opening chapter. No earlier live stage is invented within
  the same chapter.
- All initial affiliations, origins, statuses and relationship selections carry
  `source: 'chapter'`, episode 148 and an explicit chapter.
- Relationship selections describe concrete cooperation, employment or conflict
  in the cited opening scene. They do not infer family ties or treat Tyson’s
  employees’ performances as their biographies.

## Existing and returning candidates

Ladiolus was named with the class’s introductions in chapter 370 and belongs to
phase 2. In 386 Kurapika identifies her as the fastest pupil; in 388 he awakens
her Nen. Existing Troupe members retain their original dossiers. Their childhood
scenes in 395–397 do not overwrite their later-known current statuses or
affiliations. Basho investigates the suicide in 389; Izunavi considers using
Tyson’s book in 400; Melody cooperates with Kacho and Kaiser in 400. These
relationship/fact changes belong in the original dossiers, not duplicate phase-4
dossiers.

Characters seen or mentioned here were already filed in an earlier phase or
deferred there. Filed: Ken’i, the royal family, Furykov, Satobi, Maor, Longhi,
Tenftory and the earlier Nen students. Deferred, with no record: Hinrigh, Luini,
Morena, Theta, Salkov, Kanjidol, Balsamilco, Babimyna, Sakata, Bill, Sumidori,
Shikaku, the Halkenburg guards, Peuckert, Giuliano, Sarahell, Moswana, Nukuocon,
Umanma, Bucket, Hignori, Vict and Musse. An appearance or mention here does not
alter an established gate or deferral. Because Hinrigh has no record, Maizan's
only 393 exchange (with Hinrigh) is not filed as a relationship.

Steiner (phase 1's lead for chapter 384) is not named in the pinned 384
synopsis. The 389 synopsis names him only in narration ("including Steiner and
Peuckert"), and 400 likewise. He stays deferred (#1144).
