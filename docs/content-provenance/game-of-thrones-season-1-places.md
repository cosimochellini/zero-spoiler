# Game of Thrones, season 1 places: research notes

Research for #853: 23 places that a scene of season 1 or of _A Game of Thrones_
is set in. Read through the MediaWiki APIs of the lawful sources only (#725): A
Wiki of Ice and Fire for the books, the Game of Thrones Wiki on Fandom for the
show, and Italian Wikipedia for the Italian names, all CC BY-SA. No text is
copied from them. Each place's pages are under its key in
`game-of-thrones.json`.

- **What is filed.** A place a scene is set in, in either medium. Roads (the
  Kingsroad, the High Road, the barrowlands) are not filed. Battlefields are not
  filed either, with one exception: the Lannister camp, where Shae is met.
  Places only named, such as the Neck and the Blackwater, wait for a follow-up
  issue.
- **Gates.** A place opens at the first scene set there, in each medium. When
  the wikis leave the gate open, the later one is filed. The site's chapter is
  absolute: the prologue is chapter 1, so the wiki's "Chapter N" is site chapter
  N + 1. A show episode past 10 is a later season's first visit.
- **Filed here.** Each character is filed at one place. When the two media first
  show a character in different places, the show's place is used.
- **The Wall and Castle Black** are two records: the Wall opens at the first
  sight of it, and Castle Black at the first scene in the castle.
- **Regions** are the nine realms, beyond the Wall and Essos; the Wall is in the
  North. No place has an arc: the series files none.
- **Not viewed.** Neither the episodes nor the books were viewed. Every gate or
  Italian form the wikis leave open is listed under `unverified` in
  `game-of-thrones.json`, for the owner's hand check.

## Open points

### `haunted-forest`

- The weirwood grove where Jon swears his vows (show ep 7, Jon VI site 49) is
  folded into this record; AWOIAF files it as its own place, "weirwood grove".
- Italian "ranger" for the Watch's rangers is assumed from the dub, not
  confirmed.

### `winterfell`

- Cersei and Jaime are first seen in the show in King's Landing (ep 1, at Jon
  Arryn's body) but are named at Winterfell, where both records open; filed
  here.
- Lady opens in the books at Sansa I, the inn (site 16); filed here after the
  show (ep 1).
- Sandor Clegane opens in the show at ep 4 (tourney, King's Landing) but is on
  screen at Winterfell from ep 1 and opens at Winterfell in the books (site 5);
  filed here.
- Italian "parco degli dèi" for godswood and "albero del cuore" for heart tree
  are the Mondadori forms; not checked against the dub.
- Region and form labels (#853): Il Nord, Terre dei Fiumi, La Valle, Terre della
  Corona, Borgo, Pianura, Tempio, Accampamento, Locanda are assumed Italian
  forms, not checked against the dub.
- Book gate set at Bran I (site 2), not Catelyn I (site 3): the characters who
  open there are named as of Winterfell, and the chapter rides out from it; the
  first scene inside the walls is Catelyn I.

### `pentos`

- The wedding (show ep 1, Daenerys II site 12) is held outside the walls; it is
  folded into this record.

### `kings-landing`

- Book gate: Catelyn IV opens aboard the Storm Dancer in Blackwater Bay and
  lands at King's Landing in the same chapter.
- Gendry (the forge, ep 4 / site 28), Gregor and Loras (the Hand's tourney, ep
  4-5 / site 30) are filed at the city rather than at a sub-place.

### `the-wall`

- Show gate: the cold open of episode 1 shows the rangers leaving through the
  tunnel under the Wall (Fandom: they "depart from the Wall"); no transcript
  checked.
- Book gate set at Tyrion III (site 22), the first scene on top of the Wall, the
  later of 20 and 22 (owner rule: lean later).

### `winter-town`

- Italian name "Città d'Inverno" not found on it.wikipedia; dub form
  unconfirmed.
- The show only names it in season 7 ("The Spoils of War"), so no nameSaidAt.
- Ros is filed here: she is first seen at the brothel in episode 1, though her
  record opens at episode 4.

### `inn-at-the-crossroads`

- Italian name "Locanda all'Incrocio" not found on it.wikipedia (the season 1
  page says only "una locanda"); dub form unconfirmed.
- AWOIAF files Sansa I at the inn at the crossroads; the Fandom notes say the
  books stage the quarrel near Castle Darry. The book gate stays 16.
- Uses the proposed new form "inn".

### `darry`

- Italian: book-only, so the Mondadori form applies; "Darry" assumed unchanged,
  not checked against the edition.

### `castle-black`

- Show gate: the arrival at the end of episode 2 is taken to be at Castle Black
  (Fandom first names it there); the first scene the synopsis sets "At Castle
  Black" is episode 3.

### `dothraki-sea`

- Irri and Doreah open in the books at the wedding outside Pentos (Daenerys II,
  site 12); filed here after the show (ep 2, in the tent).
- Drogo's pyre (show ep 10, Daenerys X site 73) is also set on the Dothraki Sea.
- Uses the proposed new form "plain".

### `red-keep`

- Show gate: the episode 1 scene at Jon Arryn's body may already be inside the
  Red Keep; not confirmed, so the gate is episode 3.
- Renly and Barristan open in the books at the inn (Sansa I, site 16); Petyr and
  Varys at Littlefinger's place in the city (Catelyn IV, site 19). All four are
  filed here after the show (ep 3, Red Keep).

### `vaes-dothrak`

- The Womb of the World (Daenerys V, site 47) is folded into this record.

### `the-eyrie`

- nameSaidAt 1: Fandom first names it in "Winter Is Coming" (Ned was fostered
  there; Lysa's letter comes from there).
- commonWord left off: "eyrie" is a rare English word, though "nido dell'aquila"
  is ordinary Italian. Owner to judge.

### `wolfswood`

- Book gate: Tyrion II (site 14) already has the party meet Yoren in the
  wolfswood, in a line of narration. Leaned later to Bran V (38). Owner may
  lower it to 14.
- Italian "Foresta del Lupo" is from it.wikipedia's places page; whether the dub
  uses it is unconfirmed.

### `kingswood`

- source "episode": AWOIAF cites ASOS 16 (Sansa II) and AFFC 28 for visits to
  the kingswood's edge, but both chapters are set in King's Landing by their
  infobox. If the owner counts ASOS Sansa II, drop source and set the chapter
  to 160.
- nameSaidAt 5: Renly complains of the coming hunt in the Kingswood ("The Wolf
  and the Lion"). The "Kingswood Brotherhood" story in ep 3 may say it earlier.

### `lannister-camp`

- Owner exception to the no-battlefields rule (#853): the camp is filed so Shae
  has a place; the battle itself is not.
- The show’s camp in episode 7 and the books’ camp in Tyrion VIII are taken as
  one record; the books’ army first camps around the inn in Tyrion VII (site
  57), not counted as the camp’s gate.
- Region the-riverlands: neither medium names where the episode 7 camp stands.
- Italian "Accampamento Lannister" is not a dub name; the camp has no proper
  name in either medium.
- Tywin opens in the books at the inn (Tyrion VII, site 57); filed here after
  the show (owner: the show’s place wins).

### `lhazar`

- The record is the land; the place both media set is one unnamed village
  (books: a town of the Lamb Men). Owner to confirm the id and the form "town".

### `the-twins`

- Italian "Forca Verde" for the Green Fork is assumed, not confirmed.

### `great-sept-of-baelor`

- Italian: it.wikipedia's places page heads it "Il Grande Tempio di Baelor"; the
  season 1 page says "Tempio di Baelor". "Alto Septon" assumed.
- Uses the proposed new form "sept".

### `riverrun`

- Season 1 never sets a scene inside Riverrun: the siege and Whispering Wood are
  battlefields.
- The water gate is a book detail; whether the show shows it is unchecked.

### `moles-town`

- Show gate: the "Breaker of Chains" synopsis calls it "a vassal town"; the
  Fandom place page ties the scene to Mole's Town.
- nameSaidAt 4: Sam mentions the brothel in "Cripples, Bastards, and Broken
  Things" (Fandom ref GOT104).
- Uses the proposed new form "town".

### `bloody-gate`

- Italian: it.wikipedia spells it "Porta Insaguinata" (a typo); "Porta
  Insanguinata" assumed. Dub form unconfirmed.
- Season 1 skips the gate: Catelyn's party meets Ser Vardis Egen's patrol
  instead (Fandom, High Road).

### `moat-cailin`

- Season 1 sets the matching scene (ep 8, Catelyn meets Robb's army) "in the
  Neck" without naming Moat Cailin, so the show gate is season 4.
- nameSaidAt 15: first named in "The Ghost of Harrenhal" (season 2 episode 5,
  Fandom ref GOT205).
