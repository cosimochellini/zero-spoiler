# Chimera Ant phase 1 inventory

Scope: chapters 186–198 and their anime scenes (#1020). The chapter, episode and
character pages were read through the English MediaWiki API at the revisions
pinned in `sources.json`. Manga panels, licensed episode timestamps and the
Italian dub were unviewed. Cast and appearance lists identify candidates and do
not establish when a name is spoken.

**Gate rule.** A record opens at the first scene whose pinned chapter or episode
page, or the character page citing it, reports the name in speech, thought or
writing: an introduction, an address, another character mentioning it, a
journal. A synopsis that only labels a character with the wiki's name is no
evidence. An episode that adapts a manga naming scene (its page's `Adapted`
pages and summary) names it there, unless an earlier episode page already
reports the name in speech. A later gate than the true one hides a name a little
longer; an earlier one would leak it, so doubtful leads were left unused and are
noted in `hunter-x-hunter.json`.

## Italian names

Italian follows Italian Wikipedia’s _Formichimere_ list: Neferpito, Pegyi, Jeedo
(Cheetu), Zazan, Pike, Rammot, listed in `ITALIAN_TITLES`. The surveyors, Wong,
Kurt, Reina, Haruna, Pekuba, Balda, Yunju and Baro have no entry of their own
there or in the character and Hunter lists; their English names are kept in
`NAMES_KEPT`, the 2011 dub unviewed.
