# Chimera Ant phase 5 and arc coverage audit

Phase 5 covers chapters 299–318 and the corresponding scenes in episodes
127–136. The reconciliation below takes candidates from all 133 chapter and 61
episode cast lists. The ranges are appearance metadata, including flashbacks,
covers and mentions. They do not set gates. Every source revision is pinned in
`sources.json`. Direct manga pages, episode timestamps and 2011 Italian dub
audio remain unviewed.
