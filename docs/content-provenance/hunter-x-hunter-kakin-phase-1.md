## Italian names

Read through the Italian Wikipedia API on 2026-11-06:
`Personaggi di Hunter × Hunter`, `Associazione Hunter`, `Famiglia Zoldick` and
`Geografia di Hunter × Hunter`. The character list provides the Hoicoro names,
Chowrai, Tsubeppa, Loozlus, Saresare, Harkenburg, Kachow, Towchowrai, Izunabi
and Beyond Netero. The Zoldick list explicitly names Zigg Zoldick. These are
verified reference forms; no 2011 dub exists for the Kakin-era cast.

Golem, Curly and Mark have no relevant named entry in those four lists, and
specific `Name (Hunter × Hunter)` article requests returned no pages. Their
verified English names are retained under the owner’s approved policy. The
licensed Italian manga was not viewed. The verification tables cite actual list
entries for the other 23 characters, including the source’s leading spaces in
the Tserriednich and Tsubeppa list labels.
