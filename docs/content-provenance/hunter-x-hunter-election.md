chapters use unanchored pairs. Ch320 / ep137 pairs represent the same vote

**Dated changes.** Chapter 329 / episode 143 reports that Bushidora's party was
killed. Chapter 330 / episode 143 shows Teradein killed. Chapter 329 / episode
143 eliminates Cutie, Linne, Loupe and Sanbica. Chapter 330 / episode 143
eliminates Ickshonpe. Chapter 335 / episode 146 shows Pariston naming Cheadle
his vice chairman and stepping down. The chapter 340 account of Cheadle's formal
presidency lies outside #912.

**Omitted.** Barry, Rodriot, Kess, Zetsk Bellam, Gashta Bellam and Hagakushi
attend the first vote (chapter 320 / episode 137). The appearance lists and
character biographies identify them, but the scenes researched do not supply
fresh positive naming. Latoon and Kenzaki are named when Teradein tries to reach
them in chapter 329 / episode 143. Both biographies list only a mention and
neither is seen. Izunavi appears in the voting cast list, but the scenes audited
here do not establish the name; #1032 assigns his eventual filing to Yorknew
City. The first-phase examiner and Trick Tower's third examiner are descriptive
entries. Previously filed voting-scene characters (Lippo, Khara, Masta,
Seaquant, Wing, Basho, Jeitsari, Linssen, Tsezguerra, List, Dwun and others)
already have their entries and are not duplicated.
