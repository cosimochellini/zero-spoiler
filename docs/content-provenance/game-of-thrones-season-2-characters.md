# Game of Thrones, season 2 characters: research notes

Research for #1458, the characters of the second stage: HBO season 2 (episodes
11–20) and _A Clash of Kings_ (site chapters 74–143). One section per cast group
(#1455); each group's filing child adds its own.

- **Gates.** A character opens at the first explicit naming signal in each
  medium: an introduction, the name in quoted or reported speech or printed
  thought, a caption or a title card (owner, 2026-11-21). Narration and the
  credits never count. Each gate cites its naming scene (#1425).
- **Chapters.** Site chapter = 74 + the book's chapter N, prologue 0 (= 73 + the
  Ice and Fire Wiki's `chapter_number`). The order is the Ice and Fire Wiki's
  chapter navigation (`Template:A Clash of Kings`), not the Game of Thrones
  Wiki's episode pages, which contradict it for chapters 57–62 (#1455).
- **Dated steps** pin both numbers and stop at episode 20 / chapter 143. A step
  one medium alone tells carries `source`.
- **Not viewed.** Neither the episodes nor the books were viewed. A Wiki of Ice
  and Fire was read through Firecrawl only in review round 1, for the pins it
  names (Jacelyn, Hallyne, Osmund, Alayaya, Sandor), and the Ice and Fire Wiki
  has chapter pages for none of the _A Clash of Kings_ chapters cited below but
  Sansa I. Book chapters come from the Game of Thrones Wiki's episode pages ("In
  the books") and its character pages' `Bookref` citations, matched to the
  chapter navigation. Every open point is under `unverified` in
  `game-of-thrones.json`.

## King's Landing and the Blackwater (#1463)

The court under Joffrey and Tyrion as acting Hand, the city and the Battle of
the Blackwater on the crown's side. Candidates come from the Game of Thrones
Wiki's ten season-2 episode pages (Firsts, cast lists, King's Landing synopses,
"In the books") and the Ice and Fire Wiki's Sansa I character list. Bit parts
with one scene, background courtiers and descriptors are omitted, as in season 1
(owner, 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-07).

records stepped.
