# Election chronicle (#914)

Written and independently checked on 6 November 2026. Covers episodes 137–148 of
the 2011 anime and chapters 319–339: 91 bilingual stories for all 28 new
characters from #912 and 15 returning characters. The series now has 691 stories
for 182 characters.

## Sources and method

The writer read the English Hunter x Hunter Wiki’s episode pages 137–148 and
chapter pages 319–339 through the MediaWiki API. The independent verifier
re-read both language versions of all 91 stories against those pages and checked
Ickshonpe’s character biography. The per-story source and verification notes are
in `scripts/chronicle-sources-hxh.mjs`; regenerate the full report with
`npm run docs:chronicle`.

  [Episode 148 (2011)](<https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_148_(2011)>).
- [Chapter 319](https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_319) through
  [Chapter 339](https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_339).
- [Ickshonpe Katocha](https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Ickshonpe_Katocha),
  for the chapter 331 trade and its manga citation.
- The [record inventory](hunter-x-hunter-election.md) supplies the previously
  verified naming floors and Italian dub-name forms.

Prose was written for the local story units in IT and EN, with no dialogue,
source quotations or copied paragraphs. This is a fact-check record, not a
blanket rights finding or a claim to underlying series material. No new verified
wording adaptation or mandatory public-credit entry was identified. Existing
source notices and license scope remain applicable. AI assistance: Codex
(GPT-6), for writing, assembly, tests and independent verification.

Licensed manga pages, anime timestamps and direct Italian dub audio were not
inspected. Source accuracy and the prior naming evidence remain the limits of
this verification.

## Corrections during assembly and independent verification

1. Removed a proposed Koala record/story: the existing inventory excludes this
   unnamed character, whose wiki label is a species descriptor. Kite’s story
   describes the ant without inventing a name or link.
2. Removed List from Gon’s shared episode-145 story: List’s record is
   manga-only. Gon now recognizes Ging beside other Hunters, without a
   cross-source marker.
3. Restricted Ickshonpe’s episode-144/chapter-331 item trade to the manga. The
   anime synopsis establishes a cue-card address, not the same trade. Its source
   row links chapter 331 directly; the episode only orders this manga entry.
4. Clarified that Kite, rather than the koala-like ant, has the dead girl’s
   appearance in the Italian chapter-337 story.
5. Clarified that Knov and Morel discuss how to use the parking lot by phone,
   rather than suggesting that their conversation takes place there.

All remaining markers resolve to existing characters and respect episode,
chapter and source restrictions. Gon and Ging’s completed treetop conversation
waits for chapter 339, which contains the license return and remaining advice.
Nanika’s introduction waits for chapter 332 in the manga, despite opening at
episode 139 in the anime. Clook and Leorio’s medical-study scene remain
anime-only. Cheadle’s formal chapter-340 presidency is outside this work.

## Mapping comparison

Compared all 148 episode-to-chapter mappings against base `c3596622`. Two
anchors move conservatively: episode 139 from chapter 323 to 324 (the departure
wish), and episode 141 from chapter 326 to 327 (Gotoh begins the confrontation).
Both stories carry explicit chapter pins; Gotoh’s death remains at episode 142
and chapter 327. The other 146 mappings are unchanged. Full payload equality is
not expected: home and character chronicles intentionally gain stories.
