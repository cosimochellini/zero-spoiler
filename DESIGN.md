# Design System: Zero Spoiler

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The spoiler boundary is a horizon you move"**

The reader's bookmark is one line through the story. What lies before it is
drawn, named and lit; what lies after it is under fog. Every page draws that
line with the same two marks: solid and the accent for what is reached; dashed,
fog and muted ink for what is not.

The site is dark, and only dark. Four colour sets share one structure:
"Blackout" for the hub and the legal page, and one theme per series. Type,
spacing, radii and motion are shared. The pictures are line drawings made for
this project.

The owner prefers bold, dramatic, eye-catching UI with one central idea over
restrained layouts: each page states one idea at display size. This identity is
fixed (owner, 2026-11-17). Later passes refine hierarchy, spacing, states,
motion, copy, responsive behaviour and accessibility, and add no font, colour or
theme.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Dark only, with one accent per colour set.
- One display idea per page, in Bricolage Grotesque at weight 800.
- Uppercase tracked labels and mono numerals beside sentence-case headings.
- Small radii, hairline rules, no drop shadows, line drawings.

## Colors

Dark neutrals tinted toward one hue per set, with a single accent. Every value
is OKLCH. The frontmatter lists them, prefixed by set: `blackout-`,
`one-piece-`, `hunter-x-hunter-`, `game-of-thrones-`, and `tint-`. A theme
overrides every colour token, so nothing of Blackout shows on a series page.

### Primary

- **Lantern Gold** (`one-piece-accent`): One Piece's theme, "Sea Chart". Gold on
  blue neutrals, complementary on purpose.
- **Gon's Green** (`hunter-x-hunter-accent`): a leaf green on forest-green
  neutrals.
- **Ember Red** (`game-of-thrones-accent`): the theme "Ember". A true red on
  dark ember neutrals.
- **Spotlight White** (`blackout-accent`): the hub and the legal page. Nothing
  else there is chromatic, so each series card brings the only colour.

### Neutral

- **Paper, Paper 2, Paper 3** (`*-paper`, `*-paper2`, `*-paper3`): the page; a
  tile's frame and a dialog; a pressed or picked control.
- **Rule, Rule 2** (`*-rule`, `*-rule2`): decorative hairlines; the boundary of
  a control.
- **Muted, Ink 2, Ink** (`*-muted`, `*-ink2`, `*-ink`): labels and counts; ledes
  and secondary text; text and headings.
- **Accent Ink** (`*-accentInk`): text on a surface painted in the accent.
- **Focus** (`*-focus`): the focus ring.
- **Glow, Scrim, Veil** (`*-glow`, `*-scrim`, `*-veil`): overlays, and the only
  values with alpha: the bloom at the top of the page, the dark behind a dialog,
  the glass of the site bar.

### Drawing Tints

Nineteen hues (`tint-*`), shared by every series. A record's drawing takes
exactly one, for the stroke that identifies it. The same tint colours the frame
around that drawing and the soft light behind a featured one. On the places map
it also draws a reached island and writes its saga's name.

### Named Rules

**The One Accent Rule.** The accent is a line, a word, a border or a dot. The
one filled accent surface is the Save button of the bookmark dialog (owner,
2026-10-12), with its text in Accent Ink.

**The Floors Rule.** On every paper of every set, Ink and Ink 2 hold 7:1, Muted
and the accent 4.5:1, Rule 2 and Focus 3:1, and Accent Ink on the accent 4.5:1.
`src/styles/themes.test.tsx` computes them; a colour that changes keeps them.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque (with a metric-matched local fallback)

**Body Font:** Instrument Sans (with a metric-matched local fallback)

**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with a metric-matched local fallback)

**Character:** A heavy display grotesque over a plain text sans, with a mono for
numbers. All three are self-hosted.

### Hierarchy

A 1.25 scale on a 16px base, plus two fluid sizes. The frontmatter holds the
values. Its schema has no `clamp()`, so it gives Hero and Display at their
largest; the clamps are in `src/styles/tokens.stylex.ts` and
`.impeccable/design.json`.

- **Hero** (800, fluid, line height 1): the one largest line of a page. Tracked
  -0.02em to -0.035em, semi-condensed where two lines have to hold.
- **Display** (800, fluid, line height 1): a page's or a record's title.
- **Headline** (800, line height 1.15): a listing's title, section headings.
- **Title** (800, line height 1.15): group names and names in rows.
- **Body** and **Lede** (400, line height 1.6): running text, at a measure of 52
  to 66ch, and the sentence under a title.
- **Label** (600, uppercase, tracked 0.08em): the word above or beside a value.
- **Numeral** (mono, 600 to 700, tabular figures): episode and chapter numbers
  and the reader's point, often uppercase and tracked with their unit.
- **Micro** (mono): below the scale, for the filler countdown cells on a phone
  and the names of seas on the places map.

### Named Rules

**The Two Plus One Rule.** Two families and one outlier, and never a fourth.

**The Numerals Rule.** JetBrains Mono is for numerals (owner, 2026-11-17). In
the code today it also sets counts and the short uppercase lines beside a
numeral: a tile's kind, the line under a fog tile, the language code. Leave
those, and extend mono no further without the owner.

## Layout

The shell is one centred column, at most 76rem wide. Spacing is a 4pt scale
(`spacing` in the frontmatter). Siblings are spaced with `gap`; `margin` is for
optical adjustment only.

The site bar is pinned at every width: two rows on a phone, one row from 40rem,
or from 56rem on a series page. Everything else that pins, pins under it.

Breakpoints are `min-width` queries in rem, written where they are used; there
is no named scale. `.impeccable/design.json` lists each with its use. Two carry
most of the layout: below 40rem a dialog and a peek are bottom sheets and grids
drop to two columns or one; from 60rem a record's page is two columns.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, with no drop shadows. Depth comes from the three papers, hairline rules
and the overlays: the site bar is frosted glass (Veil with a backdrop blur) and
a dialog darkens the page with Scrim. `box-shadow` appears only as a flat line
that costs no layout. The z-index levels are in `.impeccable/design.json`;
dialogs are native `<dialog>` elements in the top layer.

## Shapes

Corners are nearly square: `rounded.card` for tiles and dialogs, `rounded.input`
for buttons, fields and chips. `rounded.pill` is for round controls and marks,
such as a stepper, a close button or a dot on the map. Lines are 1px for
hairlines and 2px for a control's boundary and the focus ring. A border keeps
its width in every state.

A dashed line means "not reached": the rule over a fog band, the bookmark mark
with no bookmark set, the route past the reader's point on the map.

Drawings follow the
[`line-drawings` skill](.claude/skills/line-drawings/SKILL.md), which owns their
rules. In short: one object per record in a 4:5 box, drawn in a single 2px line
with round caps and no fills, exactly one stroke in the record's tint, no faces
and no lettering, and nothing the reader has not reached.

## Components

The code is the reference; each component's comment gives its reasons.
`.impeccable/design.json` carries six of them as HTML and CSS. The bookmark
controls and the fog components are in their surface briefs
(`.impeccable/surfaces/`).

### Buttons

- **Shape:** `src/components/ui/Button.tsx` is the only button: a 2px border in
  Rule 2, at least 44px tall. `chip` is the default; `quiet` is a 44px square
  for a glyph.
- **Hover / Focus:** the text takes the accent and the border Ink. The focus
  ring is a 2px outline in Focus, reserved at rest. A press moves it down 1px.
- **Disabled:** the attribute, the cursor and 55% opacity together, as on Save
  before the draft is a real bookmark. A control that could never act is left
  out instead: the line that clears the bookmark is absent when there is none.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** `src/components/SearchField.tsx` is the only text field: Paper, a
  2px border in Rule 2, 44px tall. On focus the border takes Ink and the Focus
  outline shows.

### Navigation

`src/components/SiteBar.tsx`: the wordmark at the left edge; the pages, the
bookmark mark and the language select at the right. The current page has an
accent underline and `aria-current`.

### Motion

Three durations and three easings, in `.impeccable/design.json`: the shortest
for a control's state, the middle for the crossfade between pages and a dialog
surfacing, the longest for a band settling, a sheet rising and the fog clearing.
Movement uses `opacity` and `transform` only; a state change may also transition
a colour. A page's title never animates, because it is the largest paint. Under
`prefers-reduced-motion` spatial motion becomes a crossfade and the fog stands
still.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** write every colour in OKLCH and take it from the tokens.
- **Do** keep Instrument Sans as the body face. Impeccable lists it as overused;
  here it is fixed (owner, 2026-11-17).
- **Do** keep the uppercase tracked label above or beside a heading. Impeccable
  bans a kicker above a heading; here it is part of the style (owner,
  2026-11-17).
- **Do** set numerals in JetBrains Mono. Impeccable treats mono outside code as
  a costume; here it marks numerals by design (owner, 2026-11-17).
- **Do** mark a link inside text with an underline. The accent sits within 3:1
  of the inks in every set, so colour alone cannot.
- **Do** space siblings with `gap`.
- **Do** keep controls at least 44px tall, with the focus ring reserved at rest.
- **Do** design the phone layout first: mobile is the owner's second priority.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use `#000`, `#fff` or a grey with no hue.
- **Don't** add a font, a colour, a theme or a light scheme.
- **Don't** paint a large surface in the accent.
- **Don't** use a drawing tint for a control, a fill or text. The saga names on
  the places map are the one exception.
- **Don't** use a radius above `rounded.card` on a rectangle, or a drop shadow.
- **Don't** set a heading in italics.
- **Don't** animate a `filter` or a layout property.
- **Don't** show, name or hint at anything past the reader's bookmark, in text,
  drawings, counts or metadata. A blurred real record is still a leak.
- **Don't** spread the legal notices: they live on the legal page, and the
  footer carries one line and one link.
- **Don't** put design vocabulary (horizon, Blackout, Sea Chart) in the copy; it
  follows the `tone-of-voice` skill.
